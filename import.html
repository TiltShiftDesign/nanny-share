Import the old timesheet

Import: one-time step. This copies the hours, time off and settings from the Claude version into the new database. Sign in with your account, then tap Import. Running it twice is harmless; it just rewrites the same entries.

Holidays: adds the unpaid holidays from the nanny share agreement (Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas Eve and Day, the week between Christmas and New Year’s, New Year’s Day, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day) as no-care days through 2027. Also safe to repeat.
